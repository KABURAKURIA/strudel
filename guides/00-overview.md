# Strudel: From Zero to Lofi Hero

Welcome to this comprehensive collection of Strudel guides. Whether you are a complete beginner or looking to create complex Lofi beats, this curriculum is for you.

## Getting Started

1.  **[Beginner's Guide: The Basics](01-beginners.md)**
    -   Learn about Cycles, making sounds, and basic mini-notation.
2.  **[Intermediate Rhythms and Effects](02-intermediate-rhythms.md)**
    -   Dive into Euclidean rhythms, polymeters, and audio effects.
3.  **[Intermediate Melody and Harmony](02-intermediate-melody-harmony.md)**
    -   Master scales, chords, and arpeggios to create lush melodies.
