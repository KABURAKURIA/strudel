# 100 Kenyan Classics Vol. 5 (Strudel Guide)

This guide adds **100 more famous Kenyan tunes** to your Strudel library, focusing on **Golden Age Hip Hop**, **Reggae & Dancehall**, **Afro-Fusion**, **Benga Deep Cuts**, **Gospel Legends**, and **New School Drill**.

## How to Use
Copy the code blocks into [Strudel](https://strudel.tidalcycles.org) or your local "Kunguru Algo" instance.

---

## Category 1: Golden Age Hip Hop & Ukoo Flani
*The foundation of Kenyan Hip Hop, from Dandora to the world.*

### 1. Angalia Saa (Mau Mau)
The anthem that defined a generation's struggle.
```javascript
// Angalia Saa - Mau Mau
// setcps(0.45)
stack(
  s("supersquare").note("c3@1 eb3@1 g3@1 c4@1").lpf(800),
  s("bd, sd").struct("x ~ x ~"),
  s("hh*8")
)
```
